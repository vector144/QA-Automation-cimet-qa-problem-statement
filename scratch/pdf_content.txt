0 2 ┬╖ W H A T G E T S B U I L T
Recording and transcript
ingestion. Zero manual
handling.
The dialler pushes the recording to CRM by API, keyed on Lead ID, within
minutes of hangup. CRM sends it to transcription with speaker separation
and word-level timestamps. Transcript is stored against the lead. Nobody
downloads or uploads anything, ever.
BUILD THIS FIRST
Everything else in this brief ΓÇö the check library, the scoring engine, the
dashboards ΓÇö is dead without this pipeline. Recording and transcript
ingestion, keyed on Lead ID, with zero manual handling, comes before
anything else.
Once a transcript exists against a lead, scoring becomes a comparison
problem: transcript against script, transcript against CRM fields, transcript
against the retailer's own plan and rate card. That comparison is what the
rest of this brief specifies.

0 7 ┬╖ N O N - N E G O T I A B L E
Constraints & guardrails
We operate in regulated Australian verticals. The scoring system inherits the
same obligations as the calls it's grading.
Γû╕ Test data only
No real customer PII in any dev or demo
environment. Use the synthetic leads and
sanitised transcripts we provide until the gate is
live on real traffic.
Γû╕ Consent is a check, not an
assumption
The system verifies the recording disclaimer
was read on every call; it never assumes
consent just because a recording exists.
Γû╕ No card data surfaced
If a card number is spoken on a call, the
transcript view redacts it before anyone sees it.
The score flags the violation; the number itself
never needs to be shown.
Γû╕ No advice, no auto-correction
The system tells you what failed and exactly
where. It doesn't rewrite the sale, override
the agent, or contact the customer on its
own.
Γû╕ Scored against the rules that were
live
Every check resolves to the check-library version
in effect on the call date, not today's version.
Γû╕ Respect the override
When a human auditor overturns an
automated score, that decision is logged, not
silently dropped.
